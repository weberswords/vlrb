## Current Issues

### Beta expiration / reinstall sends users to "Add a new device"
**Status:** Investigating — fix in progress
**First reported:** May 2026

Users who uninstall the app or whose TestFlight beta build has expired are being shown the "add a new device" screen instead of being signed back into their existing account on reopen.

We've located the cause and are working on a fix. In the meantime, your account and your vlrbs are safe — nothing has been deleted. If you're stuck on this screen, please email {{ site.support_email }} and we'll help you get back in.

# Safety at vlrb

vlrb is for the people you love, so it should always feel safe to use. This page explains what isn't allowed, how to report someone, what happens after you do, and where to get help right away.

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

## If someone is in danger right now

A report reaches a person at vlrb, but not instantly. If you or someone else might be hurt, contact help first:

- **Emergency:** call your local emergency number (911 in the US).
- **Thoughts of suicide or self-harm (US):** call or text **988**, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Outside the US, find a local line at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
- **A child is being exploited:** report it to the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org) as well as in the app.

## What isn't allowed

Don't use vlrb to send or share:

- Bullying, harassment, or threats
- Violence, or encouraging someone to hurt themselves or others
- Hate toward people for who they are, such as their race, religion, gender, sexuality, or disability
- Nudity or sexual content someone didn't agree to receive
- Anything that sexualizes or puts a child at risk. We have zero tolerance for this.
- Spam, scams, or pretending to be someone else

The full rules are in our [Terms and Conditions]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}), sections 6 and 7.

## How to report someone

Reporting is private. **The person you report is never told that you reported them.**

### Report a video

1. In the chat, touch and hold the video.
2. Tap **Report Video…**.
3. Choose what's wrong, and add a note if you want.
4. Choose whether to include the video (see [Your videos stay private](#your-videos-stay-private)).
5. Tap **Send Report**.

With VoiceOver, swipe up or down on the video to find **Report video**.

### Report a person

1. In your chat list, touch and hold their name, then tap **View Profile**.
2. Tap **Report** followed by their name.
3. Choose what's wrong, add a note if you want, and choose whether to also block them.
4. Tap **Send Report**.

### Block someone

Blocking stops someone from sending you videos or friend requests. They aren't told.

- To block someone, open their profile and tap **Block User**. To report them at the same time, choose **Block and Report…**.
- To see or undo blocks, go to **Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Blocked Users**.

### Can't use the app?

Email [{{ site.safety_email }}](mailto:{{ site.safety_email }}). Tell us the person's vlrb name and what happened. Don't attach anything that sexualizes a child; describe it instead and report it to the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org).

## What happens after you report

1. **A person reads every report.** No number of reports blocks or removes anyone on its own.
2. **Urgent reports first.** We review reports about self-harm, threats of violence, or a child at risk within **24 hours**, and every other report within **3 days**.
3. **We decide what to do.** That can be a warning, removing content, or closing an account. If we act, we tell the person which rule they broke and how to ask us to look again. We never tell them who reported them.
4. **Child safety reports go further.** When content exploits a child, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires, and keep it as the law requires.

## Your videos stay private

Your videos are end-to-end encrypted. Only you and the people you send them to can watch them. Not even vlrb can.

That means we can't see a video someone reports unless they choose to share it. When you report a video, you can include it. If you do, your phone sends a copy only to the vlrb team, who review it and use it for nothing else. If you don't, we review the report from what you tell us.

## Misusing reports

Reporting is for keeping people safe. Sending false reports to get someone in trouble is itself against our rules. We keep track of how reports turn out, and we may turn off reporting for, or close the account of, someone who keeps sending false ones.

## If we acted on your account

If we removed something you sent or closed your account and you think we got it wrong, email [{{ site.safety_email }}](mailto:{{ site.safety_email }}) and tell us why. A person will take another look.

## Requests from law enforcement

We respond to valid legal requests sent to [{{ site.safety_email }}](mailto:{{ site.safety_email }}). Because videos are end-to-end encrypted, we can't provide their contents. We may be able to provide limited account information, and any video a reporter chose to share with a report.
